COMMANDS

/fill x y z x y z id [true]

Fill the area between two block coordinates, including both corners. Use descriptive block IDs to choose a block, or 0 to remove blocks. The limit is 500 blocks unless you add true.

EXAMPLE: /fill 0 0 0 4 0 4 green_lightest

Creates a 5×5 platform of green_lightest blocks at coordinates 0, 0, 0.

/circle radius height id h|s [true]

Build a hollow circular wall (h) or solid cylinder (s), centered on your block coordinates and extending upward from your feet. The minimum radius is 2. The limit is 500 blocks unless you add true.

EXAMPLE: /circle 5 4 green_lightest h

Creates a hollow circular wall with a radius of 5 and a height of 4 blocks using green_lightest.

/square distance height id h|s [true]

Build a hollow square wall (h) or solid square prism (s), centered on your block coordinates and extending upward from your feet. The limit is 500 blocks unless you add true.

EXAMPLE: /square 5 4 green_lightest h

Creates an 11×11 hollow square wall, 4 blocks tall, using green_lightest.

/sphere radius id h|s [true]

Build a hollow sphere (h) or solid sphere (s), centered at your feet. The minimum radius is 2. The sphere cannot extend below ground. The limit is 500 blocks unless you add true.

EXAMPLE: /sphere 5 green_lightest h

Creates a hollow sphere with a radius of 5 blocks using green_lightest.

/pixelart width|auto colors|blackwhite flat|wall [true]

Choose an image to build in front of you using color blocks (colors) or white, gray, and black blocks (blackwhite). Enter a width of at least 3 to scale the image proportionally, or auto to use its original dimensions. Build at your feet (flat) or upward (wall). Transparent pixels are skipped. Add true for auto or more than 500 blocks.

EXAMPLE: /pixelart 32 colors wall true

Builds a 32-block-wide colored wall from the selected image, preserving its proportions.

/teleport x y z

Move to whole block coordinates with your feet at Y. Coordinates must be within ±10,000, at or above ground, and unoccupied.

/spawn

Return to the original starting position if it is unoccupied.

/new

Reset your world after confirmation. This replaces your autosaved world.

/save [name]

Download your world as a .bound file. Optionally enter a name, or leave it blank to save as world.bound.

EXAMPLE: /save myworld

Downloads your world as myworld.bound.

/load

Open a .bound file to load a saved world. This replaces your current world.

/screenshot [name]

Download a screenshot of your world without the interface. Optionally enter a name, or leave it blank to save as screenshot.png.

EXAMPLE: /screenshot mybuild

Downloads the current view as mybuild.png.
